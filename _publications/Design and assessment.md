This study presents the design and assessment of a domestic wastewater treatment system based on a horizontal subsurface flow constructed wetland in the peri-urban settlement of El Dorado, Jarabacoa, Dominican Republic. The system, designed for 75 inhabitants, achieved average removal efficiencies of 93% for COD, 95% for BOD, 98% for fecal coliforms, and 44% for phosphorus as orthophosphate. The results demonstrate the potential of constructed wetlands as a cost-effective nature-based solution for reducing domestic wastewater pollution in the Yaque del Norte River Basin.
