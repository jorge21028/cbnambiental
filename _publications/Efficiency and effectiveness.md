This study evaluates the efficiency and effectiveness of seven domestic wastewater treatment systems based on horizontal subsurface flow constructed wetlands in Jarabacoa, Dominican Republic. The results show effective removal of organic matter, while phosphorus and fecal coliform removal remained below the required discharge standards. The study also evaluates heavy metal behavior in the El Dorado wetland and identifies constructed wetlands as a viable and sustainable option for domestic wastewater treatment in tropical communities.
